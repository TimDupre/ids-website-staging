How we help

Three phases of a dispute, each with its own expertise. All of it standing on one evidentiary foundation.

Discovery
Getting to a complete, defensible record

Identification, preservation, forensic collection, processing, hosting, and review support, including Relativity. The phase that determines what every later phase is allowed to say.

Merits
What happened, and what the record supports

Digital forensics, structured data analysis, authenticity, and reconstruction. Competing accounts tested against the artifacts rather than against each other.

Damages
What the exposure is, and how firm the number is

Forensic accounting, economics, and valuation. Scenarios rather than a single figure, with every assumption named, sourced, and testable by the other side.
The foundation under all three
Evidence Intelligence

One team managing the evidence end to end: how it comes in, how custody is kept, how testing protocols are set, how the analysis is documented, and how the report is written. Vertical expertise is only as good as the foundation it stands on, so we own that foundation rather than renting it.

Digital Evidence, Understood

The timeline already exists. Someone has to reassemble it.

Events unfolded along a timeline. They were never recorded on one. Devices, cloud services, applications, and transactional systems each hold a fragment of it, in a different format, on a different clock. Layer those fragments over one another and a shape appears that no single source could show you. That triangulation is both the challenge of digital evidence and its strength.

What is at risk

Without a foundation of evidence, a legal team can only argue the law.

A talented team without the raw material is reasoning from assumption. The theory gets built, the position hardens, and the fact that would have changed it surfaces at the eleventh hour. Get your team standing on the actual record early and you are building on a foundation instead of a house of cards.
