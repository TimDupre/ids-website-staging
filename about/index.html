Who We Are

Experts who stand behind their work, and in front of their opinions.

Founded in 2008, iDS is a professional services consulting firm specializing in Digital Forensics, eDiscovery & eDisclosure, Structured Data, Cybersecurity, Data Privacy, and Information Governance. Over 60 full-time employees across the U.S. and U.K. have worked more than 3,000 matters, from a single mobile device to cases with 450,000 plaintiffs and billions of records.

History

iDiscovery Solutions was founded in Washington, DC in 2008 by industry veterans with more than 50 years of litigation and consulting experience combined. Initial offerings included consulting, data analytics, and processing and hosting of electronically stored information, alongside expert services in electronic discovery, digital forensics, and enterprise applications.

Under founder and CEO Dan Regard, iDS has grown into a global provider of consultative data solutions, with offices in Washington, D.C., California, Georgia, and London.

Our Mission

To provide our clients with a consultative experience that follows a structured process in order to deliver customized solutions — giving them the opportunity to control the narrative and make informed decisions.

The iDS Way

Our STEPS™ framework was built to maximize efficiency, minimize disruption, and drive success on every engagement — covering conflicts, privacy, security, budget, and execution. It is integrated into the fabric of how we work: our systems, our training, our ethos.
